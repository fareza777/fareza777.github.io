ANVILORA: IDLE BLACKSMITH

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

The short version. Anvilora: Idle Blacksmith has no account and no analytics of its own. Your game progress stays on your device. The free game shows ads from Google AdMob, which needs an internet connection; an optional one-time purchase removes ads.

What we collect

We collect nothing ourselves. Anvilora is an offline, single-player game with no account system and no developer server. Your game progress is never sent to us. Two Google services work inside the app: Google AdMob, which serves ads, and Google Play Billing, which handles the optional Remove ads purchase. Both process limited device and purchase information under Google's own policies, described below.

What is stored on your device

To let you continue playing, the game keeps your progress in a local save on your device, inside the app's own private storage area. This save stays on your device; we do not receive or upload it.

Advertising

The game shows ads supplied by Google AdMob, including optional rewarded ads. To deliver and measure ads, Google AdMob may collect and process information such as your advertising ID, approximate location, IP address and app interactions, under Google's advertising policies. Consent is handled through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP): where required, a consent form is shown before ads are requested, and ads follow the choice you make there. You can review or change your choice at any time through the privacy options available in the game's settings. Watching a rewarded ad is always optional. Your game progress is never sent to Google by us. Ads are requested only when a network connection is available.

You can reset your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your Android device's settings (typically under Settings → Privacy or Google → Ads).

Purchases

The optional Remove ads purchase is a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Google Play. Payment details are handled by Google Play and are never sent to or stored by the developer. Refunds follow Google Play's policy.

Permissions

The game uses internet and network access so that Google AdMob can request ads and Google Play Billing can complete the optional purchase.

Deleting your data

All game data lives on your device. To delete it, clear the app's data in Android settings or uninstall the game. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Data processed by Google (such as advertising data) is governed by Google's policies and your Google account and device settings.

Children

Anvilora: Idle Blacksmith is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new “last updated” date.
