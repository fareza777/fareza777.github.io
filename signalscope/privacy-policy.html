F7 Developer — SignalScope

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

SignalScope – Network & Radio Tools is an Android diagnostics app that reads the radio and network information your device exposes: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth LE, cellular, GNSS, NFC and standard network tools. This policy explains what the app reads, what stays on your phone, and which third parties process data.

What stays on your device

Scan results, GATT service trees, GNSS fixes, ping and DNS results, saved history entries and your settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. F7 Developer does not operate an account system, a cloud sync service or an analytics backend for SignalScope, and cannot read your scans, saved networks, device addresses or measurement history.

You can clear individual history entries, export a report you generated yourself, or delete everything by clearing the app's data in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.

Permissions and what they are used for

PermissionWhy the app asks
Nearby Wi-Fi devices, Wi-Fi stateList visible access points, channel, band, RSSI and security type. Android requires this for Wi-Fi scanning.
Bluetooth scan / connectDiscover nearby BLE devices and read their GATT services. No pairing or data modification is performed.
Fine and coarse locationAndroid returns Wi-Fi and BLE scan results only when location is allowed, and GNSS needs it. The app does not store a location history or send your position anywhere.
NFCRead tag identifiers and NDEF payloads you physically tap. Tags are not written unless you explicitly choose to.
Internet / network statePing, DNS, port and speed tools, plus ad and billing requests.
NotificationsOptional completion notices for long-running tools.
Advertising IDUsed by the Google Mobile Ads SDK, as required when targeting Android 13 and later.

The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, call log, storage-wide or background location access. USB host access is optional and only used for SDR device identification.

Advertising and consent

The free version shows a banner, occasional interstitials at natural breaks, and an optional rewarded video you choose to watch. Ads are never shown over an active scan, a probe in progress, or a measurement screen.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share your IP address, approximate location derived from IP, app and ad interaction data, diagnostics and device or account identifiers (including the advertising ID where permitted) for advertising, measurement, personalisation and fraud prevention. That data is encrypted in transit. See Google's Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure, the Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising technologies.

Where required (EEA, UK, Switzerland and applicable US state laws), consent is collected with Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad request. Declining personalised ads does not remove ads; it limits personalisation. You can reopen the privacy options from Settings at any time.

Ads need a network connection. Offline, all diagnostic tools keep working and ad requests simply fail.

In-app purchase

The optional Remove Ads purchase is a one-time non-consumable product offered through Google Play Billing for USD 4.99. The final localised price is set by Google Play. A completed purchase removes banner and interstitial ads for that Google Play account and is restored through Google Play purchase history. Google Play processes payment, order, tax and refund information under its own privacy policy. The app receives only the purchase result needed to grant the entitlement and never sees or stores your payment details.

What the app does not do

  • It does not upload your scans, device addresses, saved networks or measurement history to F7 Developer.
  • It does not crack, unlock or join networks you are not authorised to use.
  • It does not claim to detect every tracker, and it does not measure spectrum: the SDR screen identifies USB devices only.
  • It does not read messages, contacts or files.

Children

SignalScope is a technical utility for general audiences and is not directed to children under 13. Ad requests are configured for a content rating suitable for the store listing.

Your choices and deletion

  • Change ad consent choices from the consent form or Settings → Privacy options.
  • Delete history entries inside the app, or clear all data via Android Settings → Apps → SignalScope → Storage → Clear data.
  • Manage or cancel purchases through Google Play.

For a privacy request or question, use the developer contact address shown on the Google Play listing for SignalScope.

Security

Saved readings remain in the app sandbox. No platform credentials, signing keys or payment secrets are stored in the app or its assets.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above is updated and the new version ships with the app. Material changes are reflected in the store listing where required.
