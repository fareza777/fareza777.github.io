ORBELLA

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

The short version. Orbella has no account and no analytics of its own. Your learning record stays on your device. The free app shows ads from Google AdMob, which needs an internet connection; an optional one-time purchase removes banner and interstitial ads.

What we collect

We collect nothing ourselves. Orbella has no account system and sends none of your vocabulary progress, saved words or typing results to us. Two Google services work inside the app: Google AdMob, which serves ads, and Google Play Billing, which handles the optional remove-ads purchase. Both process limited device and purchase information under Google's own policies, described below. No advertising or billing SDKs of our own are embedded.

What is stored on your device

To make learning work, the app keeps a small amount of information locally, inside its own private storage area that other apps cannot read:

WhatWhyWhere
Your display name, level, daily goal, theme, preferred realms, reminder timeTo remember your settingsLocal preferences file
Your review history — cards, intervals, streak, XP, bookmarks, typing recordsTo schedule your reviews and show your statisticsLocal SQLite database

None of this leaves your device unless you choose to export it.

Advertising

Banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads are supplied by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may process device information such as your advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, and ad interactions, under Google's advertising policies. Where required, such as in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, Orbella shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before ads are requested, and ads follow the choice you make there. Watching a rewarded ad is always optional. Your vocabulary progress, saved words and typing results are never sent to Google. Ads are requested only when a network connection is available; the dictionary and all learning features work offline.

Purchases

The optional Remove ads purchase is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Google Play. Orbella never receives or stores your payment details. The app keeps only a local flag that ads are off and checks with Google Play at launch whether the purchase is still active. Refunds follow Google Play's policy.

Permissions we ask for

  • Internet and network state — used by Google AdMob to request ads and by Google Play Billing to complete the optional purchase.
  • Notifications — used only for the optional daily study reminder you switch on yourself. If you never turn it on, the app never asks.
  • Vibration — used for the small haptic pulse when you answer a card.

Orbella requests no other permissions. It has no access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or phone identity.

Exporting and restoring

You can export your entire learning record as a plain JSON file. The file is written wherever you choose through the system file picker, and from that moment it is yours to keep wherever you like. Nothing is uploaded. Restoring works the same way: you pick a file, and it is read locally.

Deleting your data

Open Settings → Reset all progress to erase every card, streak, bookmark and achievement from the device. Uninstalling the app removes all of it as well. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

Orbella is a general-audience study tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are provided by Google AdMob under its own policies.

Third-party content

The illustrated dictionary, dataset and audio recordings bundled with the app come from the open-source Word Atlas project and are used under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence. They are shipped inside the app and are not fetched from any server at runtime. Full attribution is available on the in-app Licences screen.
