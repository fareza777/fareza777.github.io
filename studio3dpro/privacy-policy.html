Privacy Policy – 3D Studio Pro: 3D Model & Rig

Last updated: 3 November 2026
Applies to: 3D Studio Pro for Android (package com.studio3dpro.app)
Developer: F7 Developer · Contact: fajar.mreza@gmail.com

Summary

3D Studio Pro is an on-device 3D modeling, rigging and animation tool. All 3D processing happens on your device. There is no account or sign-in, no cloud sync, no analytics and no crash-reporting SDK. Your models, rigs, animations, textures and exports are never uploaded by the app.

The app uses the network only for advertising (Google AdMob), the consent message that governs it (Google UMP), and the optional in-app purchase (Google Play Billing).

Advertising – Google AdMob

The app shows banner, interstitial and opt-in rewarded ads served by Google AdMob. To serve, limit frequency of and measure ads, Google may collect or receive the device's advertising ID, device and app information, an IP address (used to estimate approximate location), and ad interaction data. Depending on your consent choices, ads may be personalised or non-personalised. Your scenes and files are never shared with the ad SDK. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent – Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

When required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and certain US states), the app shows a consent message provided by Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested. Ads are only loaded after the consent status allows it. You can review or change your choices at any time in Settings → About → Privacy options (shown when applicable). Your consent choice is stored on your device by the UMP SDK.

Purchases – Google Play Billing

The one-time "Remove ads" product is processed by Google Play Billing. The app only receives whether you own the product; it never sees your payment details. Purchases are governed by the Google Payments privacy notice. Buying "Remove ads" stops all ad requests from the app. Watching an opt-in rewarded ad pauses banner and interstitial ads for 30 minutes.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in, email address or phone number.
  • No upload of your models, textures, projects or exports.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK besides the Google advertising, consent and billing SDKs described above.
  • No access to contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, SMS or call logs.
  • No sale of personal information by the developer.

Permissions

INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (ads, consent and billing), VIBRATE (haptic feedback).

Children

The app is a general-purpose creative tool and is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Android backup

If device backup is enabled, Android may include app-private project files in your Google account backup according to your device settings. That backup is controlled by Android and your Google account, not by this app.

Your choices

  • Change ad consent in Settings → Privacy options, or reset your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Delete projects in the app, or clear all data via Android Settings → Apps → 3D Studio Pro → Storage → Clear data.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its private storage.
