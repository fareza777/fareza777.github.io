Privacy Policy – Universal Converter: 3D PDF

Effective date: 9 November 2026
Applies to: Universal Converter: 3D PDF for Android (package com.universalconverter.pro)
Developer: F7 Developer · Contact: fajar.mreza@gmail.com · Website: https://f7developer.com
This policy: https://fareza777.github.io/universalconverter/privacy-policy.html

Summary

Universal Converter: 3D PDF converts files on your device. All conversion happens on-device. Conversion files and their contents are never uploaded. There is no account, no sign-in, no login, and no developer-operated backend. The developer does not operate an analytics or crash-reporting SDK of their own.

The app uses the network only for advertising (Google AdMob), the consent message that governs it (Google User Messaging Platform), and the optional in-app purchase (Google Play Billing). Share and save-copy leave the device only when you choose those actions.

Advertising – Google AdMob

The free version may show Google AdMob ads of three kinds: an adaptive banner above the app's bottom navigation, a full-screen interstitial after several completed conversions (frequency-capped), and an opt-in rewarded ad that you choose to watch. Watching a rewarded ad grants a 24-hour period without banners or interstitials. Ads are not shown while you already have an active ad-free period or a Remove Ads purchase.

To serve ads, limit how often they appear, measure them, and help prevent fraud, Google may collect or receive:

  • device or other identifiers, including the advertising ID and other device IDs;
  • approximate location derived from IP address (not precise location);
  • app and ad interactions (for example impressions and clicks);
  • diagnostics, crash and performance information from the ads SDK.

Depending on your consent choices, ads may be personalised (personal) or non-personalised (non-personal). Your conversion files and their contents are never shared with the ads SDK by the developer. This data is processed by Google under its own policies. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, Google advertising technologies and the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent – Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

When required by law (for example GDPR in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and certain US state privacy laws), the app shows a consent message provided by Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested. Ads are only loaded after the consent status allows it. When Google makes a privacy-options entry applicable, that control is available in Settings so you can review or change your choices. Your consent choice is stored on your device by the UMP SDK. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in Android settings.

Purchases – Google Play Billing

The optional one-time product remove_ads (USD 4.99, or the local price set by Google Play) is processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles payment details and purchase history; the app never sees your card or bank information. The app only receives whether you own the product so it can confirm and restore the entitlement. Purchases are governed by the Google Payments privacy notice.

Buying remove_ads removes banner, interstitial and rewarded ads. Entitlement is stored locally in app-private data and can be restored from Google Play purchase history after reinstall. Refunds follow Google Play's policy.

Files are opened only through the Android system file picker (Storage Access Framework), the share sheet, or a drag-and-drop gesture. Selected files are copied into the app's private vault so conversion can continue reliably. The source remains on your device. None of the above is sent to the developer or uploaded to a conversion server. There is no account and no cloud copy of this data.

Sharing, saving, notifications and long conversions

When you tap Share, the Android system share sheet opens and you choose the destination. The file is exposed through a scoped FileProvider grant. “Save copy” uses Android MediaStore to write a copy to Downloads on Android 10 and later. These are local system operations that you start.

Notification permission is optional and is used for conversion progress and completion. A foreground service (data sync) keeps long batch conversions running when you leave the app; Android displays the required ongoing notification. Both operate only on local files.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in, email address or phone number.
  • No upload of conversion files or their contents, and no developer-operated backend.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK besides the Google advertising, consent and billing SDKs described above.
  • No access to precise location, camera, microphone, contacts, SMS or call logs.
  • No broad storage access. Files are accessed only through the system file picker (SAF), share sheet or drag-and-drop.
  • No sale of personal information by the developer.

Permissions

The app and the Google SDKs it uses request:

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — so advertising, the consent form and the optional purchase can use the network.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC — to keep long batch conversions running when you leave the app (local data sync only).
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — optional conversion progress and completion notifications.
  • WAKE_LOCK — used by advertising/billing SDK work; long conversions use the foreground service.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING — the one-time Remove Ads purchase.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — advertising ID for AdMob.

The app does not request precise location, camera, microphone, contacts or broad storage permissions. Files are accessed only through the system file picker (SAF).

Children

Universal Converter: 3D PDF is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising requests are tagged as not directed to children under the age of consent.

Android backup

The app disables Android Auto Backup (allowBackup="false"), so conversion files, history and settings stay on the device and are not included in a Google account backup. The app has no external account of its own, so there is nothing for the developer to export or erase on a backend.

Your choices

  • Review or change ad consent from privacy options in Settings when they are shown, or reset your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Delete conversion files and history in the app (Library/History or Settings), or clear all data via Android Settings → Apps → Universal Converter: 3D PDF → Storage (or Storage & cache) → Clear data / Clear storage. Clearing data does not cancel a Google Play purchase; Google Play can restore the remove_ads entitlement afterwards.
  • Copies you save to Downloads remain under your control.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its private storage on that device.
  • Because the developer does not operate an account or cloud save, there is no online profile to delete. Data processed by Google (ads and billing) is governed by Google's policies and your Google account controls.
