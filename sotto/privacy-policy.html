SOTTO: OFFLINE TEXT TO SPEECH

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Sotto: Offline Text to Speech (package com.sottovoice.sotto) is an offline text-to-speech app for Android developed by F7 Developer (Fajar M. Reza). This policy describes exactly what the app does with data, including the narrow cases where it uses the network.

The short version

  • There is no account, no sign-in, and no cloud sync.
  • Speech is generated on your device. Your text, documents, and audio are never uploaded to us.
  • Sotto contains no analytics, no crash reporting, and no telemetry. The upstream SDK's analytics client was removed from the source.
  • The app only uses the network in three situations: downloading a voice model you asked for, fetching an article link you pasted, and serving ads (unless you enable Offline Mode or have removed ads).

Network activity

  1. Voice model downloads. Sotto ships with the Nano INT8 model inside the app. Micro and Mini are optional downloads from Hugging Face (huggingface.co/KittenML/...) and are started only when you tap Download. Each file is verified against a SHA-256 hash built into the app.
  2. Article import. When you paste a link into "Read a link", Sotto requests that page once, extracts the readable text, stores it locally, and does not request it again. The request goes directly to the publisher's server; no proxy is involved.
  3. Advertising (Google AdMob). Unless Offline Mode is enabled or ads have been removed, the app loads banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Ads are never shown on reading screens, during narration, or during playback. Enabling Settings → Privacy → Offline Mode blocks every ad request.

Sotto has no servers of its own. There is no endpoint that receives your text, audio, documents, or usage.

Sotto does not request storage permissions. File imports use the Android system file picker, and exports stay in app-private storage until you share them.

In-app purchase

"Remove Ads" is a one-time in-app purchase (USD 4.99 or the local equivalent) processed entirely by Google Play Billing. Sotto never sees or stores payment details. After a successful purchase the app stores a local "ads removed" flag and asks Google Play to restore it on future installs. Purchase history is handled by Google under its own privacy policy.

Data retention and deletion

All app data is stored in the app's private sandbox. You can delete individual documents, audio files, bookmarks, and playlists inside the app. Uninstalling Sotto removes every file and the database. Because there is no account and no server copy, deletion is immediate and complete.

Android's automatic backup is disabled for Sotto (allowBackup="false"), so app data is not copied to Google Drive.

Children

Sotto is a general-audience reading tool and is not directed to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
