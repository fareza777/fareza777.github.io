F7 Developer — Pixel VFX Maker

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

Pixel VFX Maker – Sprite Studio is an offline sprite visual-effects studio for Android. This policy explains what the app stores on your device, which third parties process data, and the choices you have.

What stays on your device

Projects, layers, favourites, presets, appearance settings, the remove-ads entitlement cache and every rendered export are stored in the app's private storage or its app-specific external files directory. The effect renderer, editor, timeline, particle tools and all five export formats run locally and work without a network connection. F7 Developer does not operate a project server, does not upload your artwork, and cannot access your projects remotely.

Removing the app or clearing its data through Android Settings deletes local projects and preferences. Exported files that you saved to Photos/Movies remain in your gallery until you delete them.

Advertising and consent

The free version uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for an adaptive banner on the Studio, Projects and Settings tabs, a frequency-capped interstitial after some completed exports, and optional rewarded videos that unlock 24 hours of Pro export. Banner ads are placed above the navigation bar and never cover editor or export controls.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share your IP address, approximate location derived from IP, app interaction data, ad interaction data, diagnostics and device or account identifiers (including the advertising ID where permitted) for advertising, measurement, personalisation and fraud prevention. That data is encrypted in transit. See Google's Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure, the Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising technologies.

Where required (EEA, UK, Switzerland and applicable US state privacy laws), consent is collected with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before ads are requested. You can reopen the consent form at any time from Settings → Privacy & ads → Ad privacy options. Declining personalised ads does not remove ads; it limits personalisation. Rewarded videos are always optional: closing early grants nothing.

In-app purchase

The optional Remove Ads purchase is a one-time non-consumable product offered through Google Play Billing for USD 4.99. The final localised price is set by Google Play. A completed purchase removes banner and interstitial advertisements for that Google Play account and is restored on reinstall through Google Play purchase history or the app's Restore purchase action. Google Play processes payment, order, tax and refund information under its own privacy policy. The app receives only the purchase result needed to grant the entitlement and never sees or stores your payment card details.

Exports and sharing

Transparent PNG, sprite sheet with Aseprite JSON, animated GIF, H.264 MP4, project JSON and engine presets (Unity, Godot 4, GameMaker) are written locally. Nothing is shared unless you invoke the Android share sheet or Save to Photos, which uses scoped content URIs.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state — required for ads and Google Play Billing.
  • Storage (Android 8–9 only) — requested only when you explicitly choose Save to Photos. Android 10 and later use scoped MediaStore storage and need no storage permission.

The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, SMS or call permissions.

Children

Pixel VFX Maker is a creative tool for general audiences and is not directed to children under 13. Ad requests are configured for a maximum content rating suitable for the store listing.

Your choices and deletion

  • Change ad consent choices in Settings → Privacy & ads.
  • Change the theme in Settings → Appearance.
  • Delete local projects and preferences via Android Settings → Apps → Pixel VFX Maker → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.
  • Delete saved exports from your Photos/Movies gallery.
  • Manage or cancel purchases through Google Play.

To make a privacy request or ask a question, use the developer contact address shown on the Google Play listing for Pixel VFX Maker.

Security

Project data and preferences remain in the app sandbox. No platform credentials, signing keys or payment secrets are stored in the app or its assets.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated and the new version will ship with the app. Material changes will be reflected in the store listing where required.
