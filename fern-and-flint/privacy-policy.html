Privacy Policy

Fern & Flint — an idle tale of the wild and the forge

Effective 22 November 2026

The short version

Fern & Flint keeps its story on your device. We do not require an email login. A display nickname is optional for online boards. Advertising is separate from online play. Advertising is served by Google AdMob and can be removed with the one-time Remove Ads purchase; AdMob may use device information to serve ads. The save, gear and every quiet deed stay in local storage and leave with the app if you uninstall it.

What is stored on your device

The game stores your save data, settings, theme, text size and purchase entitlement in the app’s private storage. This includes your chosen keeper, name, progress, inventory and the time of your last visit. Core save data stays on your device. Optional online features upload only the public nickname and combat snapshot described below — not your email or payment details. There is no email-based game account.

Online features (Grand Arena)

Optional online features use F7 Developer’s ranking service (f7-rank-api). When you join Rankings, World Boss raids or async PvP, the game may send a display nickname you choose, a public combat snapshot (hero, power, levels and similar game stats), and anonymous device-scoped identifiers needed to keep your place on the boards. This is not a login account and is not used for advertising. You can change your nickname in Grand Arena. Turning offline or clearing the app removes local access; server-side board rows may persist until rotated by the service.

Advertising

Advertising is served by Google AdMob using the Google Mobile Ads SDK and can be removed with the one-time Remove Ads purchase. The SDK may collect device identifiers (including the Android advertising ID), IP address, coarse location derived from IP, app interactions and ad performance information, subject to your consent and Google’s policies. Consent is collected through Google’s User Messaging Platform where required. The banner appears only in the Journal, does not cover the game or Android navigation, and is removed by the Remove Ads purchase. You can revisit your choices through Settings → Ad privacy options. Learn more at Google Advertising Policies.

Purchases

The Remove Ads purchase is a one-time, non-consumable product handled by Google Play Billing. Google processes payment; F7 Developer does not receive your full payment details. The entitlement is stored locally and can be restored through Settings or the store’s restore flow.

Sharing and review

If you tap Share or Rate, the operating system or an installed store handles the action. Sharing sends the message you see in the share sheet. The in-app review prompt is provided by the store and does not give F7 Developer access to your review identity.

Children

Fern & Flint is a general-audience fantasy game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party service, contact us and we will help address it.

Data retention and deletion

Game data remains on your device until you use Settings → Forget this tale, clear the app storage, or uninstall the app. Removing ads or clearing data does not delete purchase records held by Google Play.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated policy will ship with the game and carry a new effective date. Questions can be sent to hello@f7developer.com.
