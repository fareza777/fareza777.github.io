Cozy Acres privacy.

Last updated October 11, 2026

About this policy

Cozy Acres: Farming RPG is made by F7 Developer. This policy explains what information the game handles when you play it on Android.

Information stored on your device

Your farm progress, inventory, the farmer name you choose, and game settings are saved on your device. Cozy Acres has no account system and does not upload your save data to a F7 Developer server. Uninstalling the game removes these local saves.

Advertising and consent

Cozy Acres uses Google AdMob to show optional advertising. AdMob may process your device advertising identifier, IP address, ad interaction information, and diagnostic information to deliver ads, limit ad frequency, prevent fraud, and measure performance.

  • In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, Google's consent flow asks for your choice before personalized ads are shown.
  • You can review or change the choice in the game under Settings, then Ad privacy.
  • Rewarded videos are always optional.

Read the Google Privacy Policy and Google's partner-services explanation.

Purchases

The optional Remove Ads purchase is processed by Google Play Billing. F7 Developer does not receive or store your payment details. A successful purchase removes banner and interstitial ads for the game.

Children

Cozy Acres is suitable for a general audience. The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the way Cozy Acres handles information changes, this page will be updated with a new date before or when the change takes effect.
