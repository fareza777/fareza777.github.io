Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

Anatomy Studio: 3D Engineering is an offline-first interactive technical atlas of engineered systems. This policy explains what the app stores on your device and what leaves it.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps your favourites, last opened model, appearance settings, text scale, remove-ads entitlement cache and the optional AI Lab configuration (endpoint, model name and API key) in local app storage. Removing the app, or clearing its storage from Android settings, deletes that local data, subject to your device backup settings. We do not operate a sign-in, cloud save or analytics backend.

Advertising and consent

Android builds include Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). The app may show banner and interstitial advertisements, and an optional rewarded video that grants a temporary ad-free period. An internet connection is required for ads; every 3D model, cutaway, exploded view, simulation and export works offline.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share your IP address, ad interactions, diagnostics and device or account identifiers for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. That data is encrypted in transit. See Google's Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure, the Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising technologies.

Before requesting ads, the app asks UMP for the applicable consent status, and shows the Google consent form where Google requires one. Rewarded videos are always optional: you choose to watch, the reward is exactly what the button states, and closing early grants nothing. Advertising-ID controls are available in Android settings.

In-app purchase

The optional Remove ads purchase is a one-time non-consumable product offered through Google Play Billing for USD 4.99. The final localized price is set by Google Play. A completed purchase removes banner and interstitial advertisements and the ad-free reward prompt for that Google Play account, and is restored automatically on reinstall through Google Play purchase history. Google Play processes payment, order, tax and refund information under its own privacy policy. The app receives only the purchase result needed to grant the entitlement and never stores payment details.

Optional AI Lab

The AI Lab is off by default and requires you to supply an endpoint URL and API key for a third-party image generation service of your choice. If you use it, the prompt you type, together with your key, is sent directly from your device to the endpoint you configured. We do not proxy, store or see that request. The default endpoint is an OpenAI-compatible images API; that provider's own terms and privacy policy apply. Leave the AI Lab unused if you do not want any data to leave your device.

Exports and sharing

Technical plates exported as PNG or SVG are written to app storage and shared only through the Android share sheet that you invoke. We never upload exported files.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data safety summary

  • Collected by the developer: none.
  • Collected by third-party SDKs: device or other IDs, app activity and diagnostics, shared with Google AdMob for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention.
  • Encrypted in transit: yes.
  • Data deletion: uninstalling the app or clearing app storage removes all locally stored data.

Changes
