Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Pactborne ("the game") is a single-player game. This policy explains what data is involved when you play.

Your game progress

Progress is saved only on your device. It never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its app data. We do not run servers and we do not have accounts.

Advertising (free version)

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers, approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interactions, and basic app/device information such as diagnostics.

In the EEA, the UK, and other regions where required, the game asks for consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad Privacy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in device settings. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses data from partner apps.

Purchases

The optional “Remove Ads” purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google Play tells the game only whether the purchase is active, so it can be restored on your devices.

What we do not collect

We do not sell personal data, and the game itself does not collect your name, email, contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files.

Permissions

The game uses internet access for ads and purchases, advertising ID for ads, billing for purchases, and vibration for haptics.

Children

Pactborne is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed to collect information from them.
