Privacy Policy – Ashen Hour: Action RPG

Last updated: 6 November 2026
Applies to: Ashen Hour: Action RPG for Android (package com.ashenhourdev.ashenhour)
Developer: F7 Developer · Contact: fajar.mreza@gmail.com
This policy: https://fareza777.github.io/ashenhour/privacy-policy.html

Summary

Ashen Hour: Action RPG is a local, single-player action RPG built with Unity for Android. All gameplay runs on your device. There is no account, no sign-in, no login, and no cloud-sync or developer-operated game backend. Game progress, settings, consent records and the remove-ads entitlement are stored only in Unity PlayerPrefs and other app-private storage on the device.

The game uses the network only for advertising (Google AdMob), the consent message that governs it (Google User Messaging Platform), and the optional in-app purchase (Google Play Billing).

Advertising – Google AdMob

The game may show Google AdMob ads of three kinds: a banner on menu screens only (placed in the display safe area so it does not cover gameplay or system bars), interstitial ads, and opt-in rewarded ads that you choose to watch. The ads SDK in this build is Google Mobile Ads for Unity, package com.google.ads.mobile 11.6.

To serve, limit frequency of and measure ads, and to help prevent fraud, Google may collect or receive third-party identifiers such as the device advertising ID, device and app information, an IP address (used to estimate approximate location, not precise location), and ad interaction data. Depending on your consent choices, ads may be personalised (personal) or non-personalised (non-personal). Your save data, settings and in-game progress are never shared with the ads SDK by the developer. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent – Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

When required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and certain US states), the game shows a consent message provided by Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested. Ads are only loaded after the consent status allows it. When Google makes a privacy-options entry applicable, that control is shown so you can review or change your choices, including from Settings when it applies. Your consent choice is stored on your device by the UMP SDK. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in Android settings.

Purchases – Google Play Billing

The optional one-time product remove_ads (USD 4.99, or the local price set by Google Play) is processed by Google Play Billing through Unity IAP, package com.unity.purchasing 5.5. The game only receives whether you own the product; it never sees your payment details. Purchases are governed by the Google Payments privacy notice.

Buying remove_ads removes banner and interstitial ads only. Opt-in rewarded ads remain available after the purchase. Entitlement is remembered in local PlayerPrefs / app-private data and can be restored from Google Play purchase history after reinstall. Refunds follow Google Play's policy.

Data handled on your device

There is no developer account and no cloud copy of this data. The developer cannot read your saves from a server.

What the game does not do

  • No account, sign-in, email address or phone number.
  • No cloud sync and no developer-operated backend for progress.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK. The Unity engine includes the core module com.unity.modules.unityanalytics; that is part of the Unity runtime and is not the Unity Analytics service. The developer does not send gameplay, progress or device data to a Unity Analytics or crash-reporting backend.
  • No access to precise location, camera, microphone, contacts, SMS or call logs.
  • No sale of personal information by the developer.

Permissions

INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE are needed so advertising, the consent form and the optional purchase can use the network. The game does not request precise location, camera, microphone or contacts permissions.

Children

Ashen Hour: Action RPG is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Android backup

If device backup is enabled, Android may include app-private data (including PlayerPrefs) in your Google account backup according to your device settings. That backup is controlled by Android and your Google account, not by a developer server. The game has no external account of its own, so there is nothing for the developer to export or erase on a backend.

Your choices

  • Review or change ad consent when privacy options are shown (including from Settings when that control applies), or reset your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Delete local progress, settings, consent records and the locally cached purchase flag via Android Settings → Apps → Ashen Hour: Action RPG → Storage → Clear data. Clearing data does not cancel a Google Play purchase; Google Play can restore the remove_ads entitlement afterwards.
  • Uninstalling the game removes its private storage on that device.
  • Because the developer does not operate an account or cloud save, there is no online profile to delete. Data processed by Google (ads and billing) is governed by Google's policies and your Google account controls.
