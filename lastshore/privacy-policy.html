F7 Developer — Last Shore

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

Last Shore: Island Survival is a single-player 3D island survival game for Android. This policy explains what the game stores on your device, which third parties process data, and the choices you have.

What stays on your device

Your save file, world progress, settings, discovered island stories and the local Remove Ads entitlement are stored in the app's private storage. The island, swimming, cooking, shelter building, crafting and day-night cycle all run locally. F7 Developer does not operate an account system, a game server, cloud save or analytics backend, and cannot access your save data remotely.

Removing the game or clearing its data through Android Settings deletes that local progress.

Advertising and consent

The free version uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for a banner, an interstitial and an optional rewarded video. Banner ads are placed so they never cover gameplay controls. Interstitials appear only at natural breaks with a cooldown. Rewarded videos are always optional: you choose to watch, the reward is exactly what the button states, and closing early grants nothing.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share your IP address, approximate location derived from IP, app interaction data, ad interaction data, diagnostics and device or account identifiers (including the advertising ID where permitted) for advertising, measurement, personalisation and fraud prevention. That data is encrypted in transit. See Google's Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure, the Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising technologies.

Where required (EEA, UK, Switzerland and applicable US state privacy laws), consent is collected with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before ads are requested. Declining personalised ads does not remove ads; it limits personalisation.

Ads need a network connection. Without one the game remains fully playable and ad requests fail quietly.

In-app purchase

The optional Remove Ads purchase is a one-time non-consumable product offered through Google Play Billing for USD 4.99. The final localised price is set by Google Play. A completed purchase removes banner and interstitial advertisements for that Google Play account and is restored through Google Play purchase history or the game's Restore action. Google Play processes payment, order, tax and refund information under its own privacy policy. The game receives only the purchase result needed to grant the entitlement and never sees or stores your payment card details.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state — required for ads and Google Play Billing.
  • Advertising ID — declared for the Google Mobile Ads SDK, as required when targeting Android 13 and later.
  • Vibrate — used for optional haptic feedback.

The game does not request camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, storage, SMS or call permissions.

Children

Last Shore is a survival game for general audiences and is not directed to children under 13. Ad requests are configured for a maximum content rating suitable for the store listing.

Your choices and deletion

  • Change ad consent choices in the consent form shown in the game.
  • Delete local progress via Android Settings → Apps → Last Shore → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the game.
  • Manage or cancel purchases through Google Play.

To make a privacy request or ask a question, use the developer contact address shown on the Google Play listing for Last Shore.

Security

Save data and settings remain in the app sandbox. No platform credentials, signing keys or payment secrets are stored in the game or its assets.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated and the new version will ship with the game. Material changes will be reflected in the store listing where required.
