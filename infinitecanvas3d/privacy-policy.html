Privacy Policy – Infinite Canvas 3D: Whiteboard

Last updated: 9 November 2026
Applies to: Infinite Canvas 3D: Whiteboard for Android (package com.fajar.ultimatecanvas3d)
Developer: F7 Developer · Contact: fajar.mreza@gmail.com
This policy: https://fareza777.github.io/infinitecanvas3d/privacy-policy.html

Summary

Infinite Canvas 3D: Whiteboard is a local 2D and 3D infinite whiteboard built with Godot 4.7 for Android. All boards, notes and editing run on your device. There is no account, no sign-in, no login, no analytics, and no cloud-sync or developer-operated backend. Boards, notes, imported images, PDF pages, GLB models, exports, settings, consent records and the remove-ads entitlement stay in app-private storage on the device (Godot user://).

The app uses the network only for advertising (Google AdMob), the consent message that governs it (Google User Messaging Platform, being added for the EEA, the UK and certain US states), and the optional in-app purchase (Google Play Billing). Share and export leave the device only when you choose to use those actions.

Advertising – Google AdMob

The app may show Google AdMob ads of three kinds: a banner, interstitial ads, and opt-in rewarded ads that you choose to watch. Watching a rewarded ad grants a 24-hour ad-free period. Ads are not shown while you already have an active ad-free period or a Remove Ads purchase.

To serve ads, limit how often they appear, measure them, and help prevent fraud, Google may collect or receive third-party identifiers such as the device advertising ID, device and app information, an IP address (used to estimate approximate location, not precise location), and ad interaction data. Depending on your consent choices, ads may be personalised (personal) or non-personalised (non-personal). Your boards, notes, imported files and exports are never shared with the ads SDK by the developer. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, Google advertising technologies and the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent – Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

Google User Messaging Platform consent is being added so that, when required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and certain US states), the app can show a consent message before any ad is requested. Ads are only loaded after the consent status allows it. When Google makes a privacy-options entry applicable, that control is shown so you can review or change your choices. Your consent choice is stored on your device by the UMP SDK. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in Android settings.

Purchases – Google Play Billing

The optional one-time product remove_ads (USD 4.99, or the local price set by Google Play) is processed by Google Play Billing. The app only receives whether you own the product; it never sees your payment details. Purchases are governed by the Google Payments privacy notice.

Buying remove_ads removes banner, interstitial and rewarded ads. Entitlement is stored locally in app-private data (Godot user://entitlement.json) and can be restored from Google Play purchase history after reinstall. Refunds follow Google Play's policy.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in, email address or phone number.
  • No cloud sync and no developer-operated backend.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK besides the Google advertising, consent and billing SDKs described above.
  • No access to precise location, camera, microphone, contacts, SMS or call logs.
  • No broad storage access. File selections use Android's system document picker.
  • No sale of personal information by the developer.

Permissions

The Android export and app manifests request INTERNET, VIBRATE and com.android.vending.BILLING. INTERNET is needed so advertising, the consent form and the optional purchase can use the network. VIBRATE is used for optional haptic feedback. com.android.vending.BILLING is used for the Remove Ads purchase. The app does not request precise location, camera, microphone, contacts or broad storage permissions.

Children

Infinite Canvas 3D: Whiteboard is a general-purpose creative tool and is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Android backup

If device backup is enabled, Android may include app-private data (including user:// files) in your Google account backup according to your device settings. That backup is controlled by Android and your Google account, not by a developer server. The app has no external account of its own, so there is nothing for the developer to export or erase on a backend.

Your choices

  • Review or change ad consent when privacy options are shown, or reset your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Delete individual boards in the app, or export a backup first if you want a portable copy. Share and export only happen when you choose them.
  • Delete local boards, settings, consent records and the locally cached purchase flag via Android Settings → Apps → Infinite Canvas 3D: Whiteboard → Storage (or Storage & cache) → Clear data / Clear storage. Clearing data does not cancel a Google Play purchase; Google Play can restore the remove_ads entitlement afterwards.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its private storage on that device.
  • Because the developer does not operate an account or cloud save, there is no online profile to delete. Data processed by Google (ads and billing) is governed by Google's policies and your Google account controls.
