Mirebound privacy policy

Last updated November 22, 2026

Mirebound: Offline Dungeon RPG is made by F7 Developer. This policy explains what information the game handles when you play it on Android.

No account required

Mirebound has no sign-in, no player account, and no F7 Developer profile. You can play the whole campaign without giving us a name, email address, or phone number.

Information stored on your device

Campaign progress, expeditions, roster, relic and provision state, settings, and your Remove Ads purchase state are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the game removes these local saves. We do not upload your save data to a server we operate.

Advertising

Mirebound shows advertising through Google AdMob. AdMob may process your device advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, ad interaction information, and diagnostic information in order to deliver ads, limit ad frequency, prevent fraud, and measure performance.

  • Banners appear only in the harbor hub, not during combat.
  • Interstitial ads appear only after certain expedition returns, with a cooldown.
  • Rewarded ads are always optional and only start when you choose them.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Google's consent message asks for your choice before personalized ads are shown. You can change that choice later in the game's settings.

Read the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner sites and apps.

Purchases

The optional Remove Ads purchase is processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment; we never receive or store your card number. The game stores only a local flag that the purchase is owned, and Restore Purchases checks ownership with Google Play.

Diagnostics

If the app crashes, Google Play and the advertising SDK may collect crash logs, diagnostics, and device identifiers to help identify and fix problems. We do not sell personal information.

Children

Mirebound is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings, then Google, then Ads.
  • Turn off personalized advertising in the same Android Ads settings.
  • Remove local data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game.
  • Buy Remove Ads once to stop banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads in the game.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
