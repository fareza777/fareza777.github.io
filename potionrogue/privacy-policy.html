PLAYER PRIVACY

Privacy policy

A clear record of what Potion Rogue handles, what stays on your device, what our advertising partner receives, and how Google Play processes the optional Remove Ads purchase.

Effective August 20, 2026 · F7 Developer

Short version

No accounts and no developer-operated servers. Ads are served by Google AdMob and can be removed with a one-time purchase.

01 Overview

This policy applies to Potion Rogue: Sort Puzzle RPG (the "App"), published by F7 Developer. The App is an offline, single-player game. We designed it so that your game progress and settings stay on your device.

The App does not require an account and has no chat, social features, developer-operated analytics, or remote game server. It does show advertisements supplied by Google AdMob, which is described in the Advertising section below.

02 Information we handle

Game data stored locally

The App stores gameplay progress, settings, accessibility choices, run history, and similar game state in local storage on your device. This information is used only to provide the game and resume your local progress. F7 Developer does not receive a copy of this data.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, microphone recordings, device files, or developer-operated usage analytics. The App has no developer-operated account system or backend. F7 Developer never receives your advertising identifier; it is handled by Google AdMob as described below.

03 Advertising

The App is free to play and is funded by advertising supplied by Google AdMob. Ads appear as a banner on menu screens and as an occasional full-screen ad between battles. Optional rewarded ads are shown only when you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game benefit. Ads never appear during a battle or over the puzzle board.

To serve, measure, and prevent fraud in advertising, Google AdMob may collect and process your device's advertising identifier, IP address, coarse location derived from that address, device and app information, and interactions with the ads it shows. This information is collected by Google, not by F7 Developer, and its use is governed by the How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services notice and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where consent is required — including the EEA, the UK and Switzerland — the App presents Google's consent form before personalised ads are served. You can change or withdraw that choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy settings inside the App. You can also reset or delete your advertising identifier, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android system settings.

Buying the one-time Remove Ads product turns off the banner and full-screen ads permanently on that Google Play account.

04 Google Play purchases

The App offers an optional, one-time in-app product called Remove Ads. It costs US$4.99 where that price is shown by Google Play and permanently switches off banner and full-screen advertising. All game content is reachable by playing, with or without this purchase.

Google Play Billing processes the transaction, payment method, purchase record, and any information Google requires to complete or restore the purchase. F7 Developer does not receive your full payment details. The App receives only the purchase result needed to grant or restore the in-game entitlement.

Google's handling of purchase information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and the terms shown in Google Play.

05 Sharing and retention

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information. Because the App does not send gameplay data to us, F7 Developer has no remote player profile to retain. Local game data remains on your device until you delete the App, clear its storage, or use the in-game reset option.

A Google Play purchase may remain associated with your Google Play account under Google's policies so it can be restored. We cannot delete Google's account or transaction records; please use Google Play support for requests about those records.

06 Your choices

  • You can play without creating an account or providing personal information.
  • You can decline the optional purchase and keep playing the whole campaign for free.
  • You can change or withdraw your advertising consent from Settings → Ad privacy settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising identifier in Android settings.
  • You can reset local progress from Settings or clear the App's storage in Android settings.
  • You can use Google Play's purchase controls to review or restore an eligible purchase.

07 Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children and does not knowingly request personal information from them. It does not require an account or ask for identifying details. Because the App shows advertising, it declares its audience in Google Play and configures AdMob accordingly. Parents or guardians can contact us with questions about the App.

08 Policy changes

We may update this policy when the App or its data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the current version. Material changes will be reflected here before they apply to a new App release.
