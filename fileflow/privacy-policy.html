FILEFLOW: FILE MANAGER

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

FileFlow: File Manager (package com.f7developer.fileflow) is an Android file manager developed by F7 Developer (Fajar M Reza). This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-up, no login.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no telemetry from us.
  • Your files are never uploaded to us. There is no FileFlow server.
  • The free app shows banner ads from Google AdMob. In the EEA, the UK and other regions where it is required, you are asked for consent first through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).
  • An optional one-time purchase, Remove ads, is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see your card or payment details.
  • Some features connect to the internet only when you use them: an optional on-device AI search model download, remote storage you configure, and a local "Transfer to PC" server.

What FileFlow accesses on your device

Files and folders. FileFlow asks for "All files access" (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) because Android allows a file manager to do its job only with that permission. It is used to:

  • list, open, copy, move, rename, compress, extract and delete files you choose;
  • read file names, sizes and dates and, on your device, file contents to build search indexes, find duplicates and junk, inspect file types, remove metadata from copies, and keep versions;
  • run the Auto Rules and folder syncs you created, on the schedule you set.

Photos and documents (on-device search). Name search stores a row per file. Content search stores text extracted from readable documents (including PDFs) and small numeric vectors used to find similar documents. Photo search stores text recognised in photos, image labels and, only if you download the optional model, photo vectors. All of this stays in the app's private database on your device, can be rebuilt or deleted, and is never uploaded.

Installed apps list. The APK backup screen lists apps installed on your device so you can save their installers as files. The list is shown on the screen and is not sent anywhere.

Storage statistics. Used to show free space and what is using it.

Network connections

Browsing, rules, cleanup and local file operations never need a connection. FileFlow uses the network only for:

  1. Ads (Google AdMob). See "Advertising and consent" below.
  2. Google Play Billing. For the optional Remove ads purchase. See "In-app purchase" below.
  3. Optional on-device AI search model download. If you choose "meaning search" for photos, FileFlow downloads a model of roughly 400 MB, which runs entirely on your device afterwards. It is fetched over HTTPS from Hugging Face (huggingface.co): the MobileCLIP2 model file from the anton96vice/mobileclip2_tflite repository and the CLIP vocabulary and merge files from the openai/clip-vit-base-patch32 repository. The download is a plain file request: no file, photo or personal data is sent, but, as with any web request, the host sees your IP address and is subject to its own privacy policy (https://huggingface.co/privacy). On-device text and label recognition by Google ML Kit may also fetch its own models through Google Play services the first time you use it.
  4. Remote storage you configure (optional). If you add a WebDAV, S3, SFTP or SMB location, the files you choose travel directly between your device and that server, using the address and credentials you enter. The developer never sees them. Saved credentials are encrypted with a key in the device's keystore where the device provides one.
  5. Transfer to PC (optional). When you start the transfer server, devices on your local network that have the link or QR code (protected by a random token in the link) can browse, download and upload files in the folder you share. It runs only while you have started it and stops when you stop it. Nothing goes through the internet or through us.

There is no FileFlow cloud and no developer server. Nothing is sent to us.

Advertising and consent

The free version shows a small banner ad on the Home, Tools and Settings screens. Ads never appear in the file browser, search results, previews, or anywhere you are working with files.

Ads are served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information and ad interactions, as described in Google's policies (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy). FileFlow declares the AD_ID permission for this purpose only. FileFlow itself does not receive or store this data.

Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and US states). Where required by law, such as under the GDPR/ePrivacy rules in the European Economic Area and the UK, or under US state privacy laws such as the CCPA/CPRA, FileFlow asks for your choice through Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested. If you decline, ads are not requested. Where Google requires it, Settings shows an ad choices entry ("Change ad choices") so you can review or withdraw your choice at any time. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings (usually Settings > Google > Ads).

If you buy Remove ads, FileFlow stops requesting ads and no longer starts the consent form or the ad SDK on that device.

In-app purchase (Google Play Billing)

Remove ads is a one-time, non-subscription purchase handled entirely by Google Play. Your payment method and billing details are processed by Google and are never visible to or stored by FileFlow or the developer. The app only asks Google Play whether your Google account owns the product and remembers the answer on your device so ads stay off without a connection. "Restore purchases" in Settings re-checks it, for example on a new phone. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy; if a purchase is refunded, ads return the next time the app checks with Google Play.

What FileFlow does not do

  • No account and no developer-side storage of your information.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or telemetry.
  • No upload of your files, photos or search indexes to the developer.
  • No access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location or calendar.

Where your data lives and how to delete it

Everything FileFlow remembers - rules, history, trash index, settings, search indexes, photo vectors, version store, sync jobs, remote-storage credentials and the remembered purchase - lives in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings removes it. You can also clear rule history (Rules > History), delete the downloaded model (photo search) and delete stored versions (Versions) inside the app.

Files you delete go to FileFlow Trash, a hidden .fileflow/trash folder on your own storage, and are removed permanently after the retention period you choose in Settings (30 days by default) or when you empty the trash. Because it sits on your storage rather than in the app's private folder, empty it before uninstalling if you want those files gone.

Because the developer does not receive or store personal data, there is nothing held by the developer to delete. Data processed by Google (advertising and billing data) is governed by Google's policies and your Google account controls (https://myaccount.google.com).

Permissions and why each one exists

Permission Why
All files access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) Browse and organise files outside the app's own folder - the core function of a file manager.
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (up to Android 12L) / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (up to Android 10) The same access on older Android versions.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Optional. Shows that a scheduled rule finished, the transfer server is running, or a model download or photo-reading pass is in progress.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Re-arms your scheduled rules and folder syncs after the device restarts.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC Keeps the transfer server, the model download and photo reading running while the app is not on screen.
INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Ads, Google Play Billing, remote storage you configure, the local transfer server and model downloads you start.
com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID Used only by Google AdMob for advertising.

Children

FileFlow is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Ad requests are not tagged for child-directed treatment because the app is not designed for children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK, or US states such as California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of personal data. The developer holds no personal data about you, so requests about advertising data should go to Google through your Google account and ad settings. You can write to us any time with questions.
