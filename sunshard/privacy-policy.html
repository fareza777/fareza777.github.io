Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

Sunshard: The Sundered Crown is an offline-capable single-player fantasy role-playing game. This policy explains what the app stores on your device and what leaves it.

Data stored on your device

Save files, settings, the remove-ads entitlement cache and the local concept-art gallery state are stored in local app storage. Removing the app, or clearing its storage from Android settings, deletes that local data, subject to your device backup settings. The game has no sign-in, no cloud save and no developer-operated analytics backend.

Advertising and consent

Android builds include Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). The game may show a banner during gameplay, an interstitial when you return to the title after several minutes, and an optional rewarded video that unlocks a concept-art gallery. An internet connection is required for ads; the game itself is playable without one.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share your IP address, ad interactions, diagnostics and device or account identifiers for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. That data is encrypted in transit. See Google's Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure, the Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising technologies.

Before requesting ads, the app asks UMP for the applicable consent status and shows the Google consent form where Google requires one. Rewarded videos are always optional: you choose to watch, the reward is exactly what the button states, and closing early grants nothing. Privacy choices can be reopened from the game's Store & extras screen. Advertising-ID controls are available in Android settings.

In-app purchase

The optional Remove Ads purchase is a one-time non-consumable product offered through Google Play Billing for USD 4.99. The final localized price is set by Google Play. A completed purchase removes banner and automatic interstitial advertisements for that Google Play account and is restored on reinstall through Google Play purchase history; opt-in rewarded videos remain available. Google Play processes payment, order, tax and refund information under its own privacy policy. The app receives only the purchase result needed to grant the entitlement and never stores payment details. Entitlement is verified on the device through Google Play Billing.

AI features and third-party endpoints

Any optional AI-assisted feature requires an endpoint and key that you supply yourself. Nothing is included, nothing is free, and no AI feature is required to play. If you configure one, the text or image you submit is sent to the provider you chose under that provider's own terms; the developer never receives it.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13. It contains fantasy combat against monsters, in-app purchases and advertising.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above is updated and the new version is published at this address.
