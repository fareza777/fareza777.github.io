Privacy Policy – Pixel Diorama: City Builder

Effective date: 14 November 2026
Applies to: Pixel Diorama: City Builder for Android (package com.f7developer.pixeldiorama)
Developer: F7 Developer · Contact: fajar.mreza@gmail.com · Website: https://f7developer.com
This policy: https://fareza777.github.io/pixeldiorama/privacy-policy.html

Summary

Pixel Diorama: City Builder is an offline city- and village-building game. Your game runs and saves entirely on your device. There is no account, no sign-in, no cloud save and no developer-operated server. The developer does not use an analytics or crash-reporting SDK of their own.

The app uses the network only for advertising (Google AdMob), the consent message that governs it (Google User Messaging Platform), and the optional one-time Remove Ads purchase (Google Play Billing). The Share and Rate buttons hand off to Android or Google Play only when you tap them.

Advertising – Google AdMob

The free game may show Google AdMob ads of three kinds: a banner at the bottom of the screen during play, an occasional full-screen interstitial at natural breaks such as travelling between islands (frequency-capped, and none in the first minutes of a session), and opt-in rewarded ads that you choose to watch for an in-game bonus. Buying Remove Ads turns off the banner and interstitial ads; rewarded ads remain available only if you choose to watch them.

To serve ads, limit how often they appear, measure them and help prevent fraud, Google may collect or receive:

  • device or other identifiers, including the advertising ID;
  • approximate location derived from IP address (not precise location);
  • app and ad interactions (for example impressions and clicks);
  • diagnostics and performance information from the ads SDK.

Depending on your consent choices, ads may be personalised or non-personalised. The developer does not share your save data with the ads SDK. This data is processed by Google under its own policies. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, Google advertising technologies and the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent – Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

Where required by law (for example GDPR in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and certain US state privacy laws), the app shows a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform so you can choose how ads use your data. When Google makes privacy options applicable in your region, an Ad privacy button in Settings and in the main menu lets you review or change your choices at any time. Your consent choice is stored on your device by the UMP SDK. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in Android settings.

Purchases – Google Play Billing

The optional one-time product remove_ads (USD 4.99, or the local price shown by Google Play) is processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles payment details and purchase history; the app never sees your card or bank information. The app only receives whether your Google account owns the product, so it can acknowledge the purchase, switch ads off and restore it later with Restore purchases in Settings. Purchases are governed by the Google Payments privacy notice.

Android backup

The app allows Android's standard backup, so if backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the game's local save in your device backup and restore it on a new device. This backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings; the developer cannot access it. You can turn backup off in Android settings.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in, email address or phone number.
  • No cloud save, multiplayer or developer-operated backend.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK besides the Google advertising, consent and billing SDKs described above.
  • No access to precise location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, SMS or call logs.
  • No sale of personal information by the developer.

Permissions

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — so ads, the consent message and the optional purchase can use the network. The game itself plays offline.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING — the optional one-time Remove Ads purchase.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — advertising ID for AdMob, subject to your consent and Android settings.
  • VIBRATE — optional haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.
  • WAKE_LOCK and similar normal permissions added by Google's ads and billing SDKs for their own operation.

Children

Pixel Diorama: City Builder is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help.

Your choices

  • Review or change ad consent with Ad privacy in Settings when it is shown, or reset your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Remove ads with the optional one-time purchase, and restore it with Restore purchases.
  • Erase your game with Delete Data in Settings, or clear all data via Android Settings → Apps → Pixel Diorama → Storage → Clear storage. Clearing data does not cancel a Google Play purchase; you can restore Remove Ads afterwards.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its private storage on that device.
  • Because there is no account or cloud save, there is no online profile for the developer to export or delete. Data processed by Google (ads, billing, backup) is governed by Google's policies and your Google account controls.
