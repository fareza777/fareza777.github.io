F7 Developer — Wildsuit

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

Wildsuit: Card Roguelike is an offline monster-card roguelike for Android. This policy explains what the game stores on your device, which third parties process data, and the choices you have.

What stays on your device

Your run progress, unlocked content, bestiary, achievements, settings, daily-trial state and the local Remove Ads entitlement are stored in the app's private storage. The game engine, battles, forging, fusing and the Night Market all run locally. F7 Developer does not operate an account system, a game server, cloud save or analytics backend, and cannot access your save data remotely.

Removing the app or clearing its data through Android Settings deletes that local progress.

Advertising and consent

The free version uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for three optional placements:

  • an adaptive banner on the title menu, above the Android navigation bar — never during a run or on a sheet;
  • an interstitial after a finished run when you tap Main Menu, with a 90-second cooldown — never in Training;
  • an optional rewarded video that grants one free reroll in the Night Market, once per shop.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share your IP address, approximate location derived from IP, app interaction data, ad interaction data, diagnostics and device or account identifiers (including the advertising ID where permitted) for advertising, measurement, personalisation and fraud prevention. That data is encrypted in transit. See Google's Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure, the Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising technologies.

Where required (EEA, UK, Switzerland and applicable US state privacy laws), consent is collected with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before ads are requested. Declining personalised ads does not remove ads; it limits personalisation. Rewarded videos are always optional and are never required to progress.

Ads need a network connection. If the network is unavailable, the game remains fully playable and ad entries fail quietly.

In-app purchase

The optional Remove Ads purchase is a one-time non-consumable product offered through Google Play Billing for USD 4.99. The final localised price is set by Google Play. A completed purchase hides the banner and interstitial placements and hides the rewarded entry point for that Google Play account. Google Play processes payment, order, tax and refund information under its own privacy policy. The app receives only the purchase result needed to grant the entitlement and never sees or stores your payment card details.

Purchase validation is handled by RevenueCat, which processes an anonymous app user ID, purchase receipt data and platform information to confirm and restore the entitlement. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state — required for ads and Google Play Billing.
  • Advertising ID — declared for the Google Mobile Ads SDK, as required when targeting Android 13 and later.
  • Vibrate — used for optional haptic feedback.

The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, storage, SMS or call permissions.

Children

Wildsuit is a strategy card game for general audiences and is not directed to children under 13. Ad requests are configured for a maximum content rating suitable for the store listing.

Your choices and deletion

  • Change ad consent choices in the in-app privacy options shown by the consent form.
  • Delete local progress via Android Settings → Apps → Wildsuit → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the game.
  • Manage or cancel purchases through Google Play.

To make a privacy request or ask a question, use the developer contact address shown on the Google Play listing for Wildsuit.

Security

Save data and settings remain in the app sandbox. No platform credentials, signing keys or payment secrets are stored in the app or its assets.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated and the new version will ship with the game. Material changes will be reflected in the store listing where required.
