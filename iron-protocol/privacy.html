Iron Protocol privacy

Last updated November 19, 2026

Iron Protocol: Robot FPS is a single-player sci-fi shooter made by F7 Developer for Android. This page explains what the game does with your information.

No account, no server

Iron Protocol has no sign-in, no account and no F7 Developer server. Campaign progress, settings, control layout and the record of your Remove Ads purchase are stored on your device. Uninstalling the game removes them.

Advertising

The game shows optional ads through Google AdMob: a banner on menu screens, an interstitial at natural breaks between missions, and a rewarded video only when you choose to revive after dying. Ads support development and the game is fully playable without watching rewarded ads.

  • AdMob may process your device advertising identifier, IP address, approximate region, ad interaction data and diagnostic information to deliver ads, limit frequency, prevent fraud and measure performance.
  • In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, Google's consent form asks for your choice before personalised ads are shown. You can change it later from the game's settings.

Remove Ads purchase

The one-time "Remove Ads" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. F7 Developer never sees or stores your payment details. The purchase removes banner and interstitial ads permanently; a record of the purchase is kept on your device and can be restored through Google Play on a new device.

What the game does not do

  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files are read.
  • No profile, email address or phone number is collected.
  • No gameplay analytics are sent to F7 Developer.

Children

Iron Protocol is a science-fiction action game intended for a general teen and adult audience. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If how the game handles information changes, this page will be updated with a new date before the change takes effect.
