ZIPHATCH: ZIP & RAR EXTRACTOR

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

ZipHatch: ZIP & RAR Extractor (package com.hatchstudio.archive) is an Android app for opening, extracting and creating archives, developed by F7 Developer (Fajar M Reza). This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-up, no login.
  • No analytics and no servers of our own. ZipHatch itself sends nothing about you or your files to the developer.
  • Your archives, extracted files and archive passwords stay on your device.
  • File access uses Android's Storage Access Framework only, for files and folders you choose. ZipHatch does not request "All files access".
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob. You can remove them with a one-time purchase.

Files and passwords

ZipHatch reads and writes only the files and folders you pick through the system file picker. Archive passwords you type are used on the device to open that archive and are never uploaded. App settings and your ad-free status are stored locally in the app's private storage and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). To serve, measure and protect ads, Google may collect and share:

This data is processed by Google under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings.

Consent

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states), ZipHatch uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent choices before ads are requested. You can change your choices later from the app's privacy options where available.

In-app purchase

"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase (USD 4.99 or the local equivalent) processed by Google Play Billing. Payment details are handled entirely by Google Play; ZipHatch receives only confirmation that you own the purchase. When it is owned, ZipHatch does not load ads or the consent form.

Children

ZipHatch is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
