Pixel Game Master · Undersun

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

Undersun stores your run progress, unlocks, settings, remove-ads entitlement cache and reward receipts on your device. The game has no sign-in, cloud save or developer-operated analytics backend. We do not send your save file or run history to an advertising service. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data, subject to your device's backup settings.

Advertising and consent

Android builds include Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). The game may show banner, interstitial and optional rewarded advertisements. An internet connection is required for ads; the game can otherwise be played offline.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share your IP address, ad interactions, diagnostics and device/account identifiers for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Data sent by that SDK is encrypted in transit. See Google's SDK data disclosure, Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising technologies.

The game asks UMP for the applicable consent status before requesting ads. Where Google requires a consent form, it is presented before ad requests. Device advertising-ID controls are available in Android settings.

Rewarded videos are optional and clearly labelled. Declining or closing a video early does not remove progress or charge the player.

In-app purchase

The optional Remove ads non-consumable purchase is offered through Google Play Billing for USD 4.99 (the final localized price is set by Google Play). A successful purchase removes banner and interstitial advertisements on the Google Play account; optional rewarded videos remain available because the player chooses to watch them. Google Play processes payment, order, tax and refund information under its own privacy policy. The game receives only the purchase result needed to grant the entitlement; it does not store payment details. Restore purchase uses Google Play's account-level purchase history.

Data safety summary

The game has no account, sign-in, contacts, camera, microphone, precise location, cloud save or developer analytics backend. Advertising and billing data are handled by Google as described above. Run progress and purchase entitlement state are stored locally on the device.

External links and support

Share, rating and privacy links open services outside the game, which have their own privacy practices. The game does not read your contacts or ask for camera, microphone or precise-location access.

For a privacy question, contact the publisher at fajar.mreza@gmail.com. We may update this policy when the game's data practices change; the current policy is linked from the Google Play listing.
