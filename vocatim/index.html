Offline control

Download a speech model, then transcribe on your Android device without a connection. In offline mode, audio is processed locally. Speed and accuracy depend on your model, device, and recording.

No Vocatim account required

Online flexibility

Connect Groq or OpenAI using your own API key. Selected audio is sent to that provider for transcription. Internet access is required; provider charges, quotas, and privacy terms apply.

Clear pricing

The free app is supported by ads. An optional one-time Google Play purchase removes in-app ads. Online provider usage is separate and is not included in ad removal.

Built for real recordings

Import audio or video, record with the screen off, recover long transcription jobs, organize notes by folder or calendar, and return to the exact spoken moment during playback.

From audio to useful notes

Create AI summaries and meeting minutes with a downloaded local model or optional configured online AI. Export TXT, Markdown, PDF, SRT, or VTT. Review AI-generated text before relying on it.

Your notes, protected

Use device security to lock the app or notes, choose dark mode and colors, and make password-protected encrypted backups of transcripts, titles, and settings. Audio files are not included in backups.
